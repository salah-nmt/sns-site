How we work

We learn your business first. Then we build.

Most automation fails because it is built from a sales call instead of from the work itself. We do it the other way round: we come to you, learn how everything runs and how you want it done, and only then start building.

  1. 01

    We come to you and listen

    We sit with the people who do the work today, on site or by video. We map every step, every tool, every exception, and every shortcut that never made it into a manual.

  2. 02

    We agree how you want it done

    Your tone of voice, your rules, your limits. What the agent may decide on its own, what it must check first, and what always goes to a person. Nothing is assumed.

  3. 03

    We build and test on your real work

    The agent runs alongside your team on real cases before it takes anything over. You see every output and approve it. It goes live when it has earned it.

  4. 04

    We go live and stay on

    Once it performs, it takes over. We monitor it, report in results you can check, and adjust it as your business changes.

  5. 05

    Then the next role

    Most clients start with one role. Once it runs, the next one goes faster, because the groundwork is already in place.

What changed

  • The website became a sales channel. Customers configure their installation, see a fixed price, book a date and pay online. That channel did not exist before.
  • Quotes go out automatically. The price on screen, the quote PDF and the payment come from the same source, so they can never drift apart and nobody has to write a quote by hand.

  • A business track was added. Property managers, building administrators and contractors go through their own enquiry flow with their own follow‑up, separate from private customers.

Questions we get asked

Before you book a call.

Will an agent make mistakes?

Any system can, and so can any employee. That is why every agent works within rules you approved, is tested on your real cases before it takes anything over, and hands anything uncertain to a person. Everything it does is logged, so a mistake is visible and fixed instead of hidden.

What happens to our data?

Agents work inside your own accounts and tools. Your customer data, your CRM and your ad accounts stay in your name. If we ever stop working together, you keep everything and we remove our access.

Do we have to change our software?

Usually not. We build on what you already use, such as Teamleader, Shopify, Klaviyo, Stripe, Make and your accounting software. Where a tool really holds you back, we tell you why before we change anything.

What does it cost?

That depends on the role and on how your business runs, which is exactly why we start by learning it. After the first visit you receive a fixed proposal: what the agent will do, what it costs, and what it should deliver. No surprises afterwards.

How quickly can an agent be live?

It depends on the role and the tools involved. You get a timeline in the proposal, and the agent only takes over once it has proven itself on your real work.

Do you work with companies in my country?

Yes. We work with companies wherever they are. The first visit happens on site or by video, the agents work in the languages your customers speak, and they run in the cloud, so distance makes no difference to how they perform.

Is this only for large companies?

No. Most of our clients are owner‑led businesses, where one missed enquiry or one late invoice is felt straight away. That is where an agent makes the most visible difference.
